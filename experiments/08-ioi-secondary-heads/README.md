# Repeating IOI interventions at two other heads

[All experiments](../../README.md#all-20-experiments-at-a-glance) · [Connections between studies](../README.md) · [Reproduction](../../docs/reproducing.md)

Original ID **E18** · GPT-2 · IOI · Outcome: **mixed**

Repeat the crossed-continuation measurements at L9H6 and L10H0.

## Recorded result

Matching advantages are about 0.025 and 0.008 nat. Both retained intervals exclude zero; the L10H0 interval does not establish an effect above the conventional 0.01-nat margin.

## Interpretation

They help show the scale and variability of the primary IOI result. Failure to exceed a margin is not evidence of absent specificity.

## Limits

These use the same GPT-2 checkpoint and source families. They are intervention replications, not independent model or research replications. The practical margin has no independent utility calibration.
